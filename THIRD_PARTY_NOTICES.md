# Third-party notices and exclusions

The licenses in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) cover the project's own eligible contributions, not the external resources described by the catalog. A link, summary, access request, or research copy does not transfer the underlying owner's rights to this project.

## Referenced resources

Resource identifiers `R01` through `R12` retain their external ownership and applicable terms. The [resource cards](docs/resources/README.md), [primary-source register](docs/reference/sources.md), and [rights and access table](docs/reference/rights-and-access.md) preserve the research trail. They are descriptions and dated assessments, not new permission grants.

The permission and clarification gates in those records remain in force. Adding our project licenses does not resolve conflicting upstream notices, authorize restricted sharing, or license a downloaded dataset as our original content. No third-party dataset binaries, external source-code packages, standards documents, or training publications are added by this licensing change.

## Standard license texts

[CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) contains Creative Commons' standard public license, whose text is dedicated to the public domain under its stated dedication. Source: [Creative Commons legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.txt).

[Apache 2.0](LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt) contains the standard Apache license, including its appendix. Source: [Apache Software Foundation license text](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.txt). Do not replace the appendix's illustrative placeholders with a project-specific grant; our copyright and scope notices are supplied separately.

## Adding external material later

Before any permitted inclusion, record the material's exact path, original author or rights holder, source and version, governing license or written grant, modifications, and required attribution. Preserve upstream notices alongside the material. Keep private permission correspondence and personal data outside the public repository.

Where a source has no verified redistribution grant, retain a metadata link rather than copying it. The project license does not override separately identified third-party terms. Follow [DATA_POLICY.md](DATA_POLICY.md) for acquisition and publication review.
